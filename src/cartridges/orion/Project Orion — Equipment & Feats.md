# Project Orion — Equipment & Feats

*Canonical Equipment, Hardware & Feat Specification (Classless Action-RPG Architecture)*

> **Design Principle — Guide, Never Enforce:**  
> Project Orion has no player-facing classes or class restrictions. Internal archetype kits (Assault, Siege, Vanguard, Recon, Artillery, Support) exist solely behind the scenes to guide item design and provide optional **Starting Loadout Kits** at Pilot Creation. Every weapon, coreplate, reactor, utility module, sensor array, actuator set, and feat is open to any pilot and gated solely by **Pilot Skill ranks**, **Gemstone sockets**, and **equipped Hardware / Weapon types**.

## 7. Frame Feats (97 Canonical Feats across Tiers I–V + Relic Feats)

All feats are unlocked by **Pilot Skill Ranks** (`Tier I: Lv 2`, `Tier II: Lv 5`, `Tier III: Lv 9`, `Tier IV: Lv 14`, `Tier V: Lv 18`), socketed with **Gemstones** (retained in build and refunded on unsocket), and checked against **equipped Weapon Family / Hardware**—never restricted by class.

- **Tier I (17 Feats — Skill Lv 2)**: Foundation hardware calibration, load step-ups (`Load-Bearing Frame`), and primary weapon family tuning (`Burst Discipline`, `Rotary Spin-Up`, `Mana Cleave`, `Close-Quarters Burst`, `Focused Emitter`, `Adaptive Calibration`, etc.).
- **Tier II (22 Feats — Skill Lv 5)**: Secondary weapon specialization, shield/barrier coupling, thermal venting, and heavy chassis clearance (`Heavy Anchor`, `Suppressive Barrage`, `Blast Salvo`, `Radiant Barrier`, `Suppressed Fire`, `Prism Focus`, `Purifying Beam`, etc.).
- **Tier III (24 Feats — Skill Lv 9)**: Mid-tier ordnance, reactor feedback loops, stealth/phase maneuvers, and multi-target harmonics (`Shock Ballista`, `Avalanche Volley`, `Judgment Lance`, `Shadowfang Precision`, `Sparkstrike Overdrive`, `Litany of Mending`, `Thermal Bleed Louvers`, etc.).
- **Tier IV (20 Feats — Skill Lv 14)**: High-commitment siege configurations, super-heavy load clearance (`Fortress Stance`), and reactor-linked weapon overclocks (`Turbine Overclock`, `Tomahawk Salvo`, `Aegis Charge`, `Dual Monomolecular`, `Manacore Detonation`, `Sanctuary Pulse`, etc.).
- **Tier V (14 Feats — Skill Lv 18)**: Capstone frame synthesis (`Titan's Grip`, `Alchemic Superconductor`, `Fathomless Juggernaut`, `Immortal Bastion`, `Phantom Assassin`, `Infinite Convergence`, `Archon of Grace`, `Æther-Synced Core Conduit`, etc.).
- **Special Relic Feat**: `Knight's Oath` — Earned by defeating Percival, Tristan, and Bors simultaneously (+25% permanent damage vs. Camelot forces).
